                                    Abstract

      A working hypothesis is proposed that combines the common core of sev-
  eral earlier constructions: causal microstructure, local proper time, discrete
  U (1) holonomies, optical observables, and thermodynamic coarse-graining.
  The fundamental kinematics is specified by a locally finite causal structure
  with phase transports on oriented links. In an appropriate continuum limit,
  causal order and volume information should determine a Lorentzian met-
  ric, while the phase transports should determine a U (1) connection. Light
  is treated as an excitation of the connection and as a null probe of the full
  geometry, rather than as the material of space or as a stretched string.
      Two competing interpretations of the phase layer are fixed. Under hy-
  pothesis HR , the layer is a physical five-dimensional geometry and, after
  reduction, contains a dynamical radion. Under hypothesis HP , the circle is
  an internal phase space over each point of the four-dimensional base; there
  is no free radion and no physical KK tower. The minimal limit of HP coin-
  cides with the Einstein–Maxwell system, and any possible new physics can
  reside only in a correlation functional generated by the joint coarse-graining
  of causal and phase variables.

  while “synchronization” may mean a decrease of relative entropy toward

  of common response parameters suitable for constructing a mathematical
  model, or a quantitative refutation of both branches.

12 Galactic Status

The original Tully–Fisher relation uses luminosity as an observational proxy. Its
baryonic form relates the asymptotic velocity to the total baryonic mass:

                                        Mb ∝ Vf4 .                           (59)

The radial acceleration relation imposes a more local condition,

                                  gobs (r) = F(gbar (r)) .                   (60)

These relations do not establish the primacy of luminosity, acceleration, or the
electromagnetic field. In the present hypothesis, they are treated as possible
boundary conditions on Γcorr .
In the weak-field regime,

                       ds2 = −(1 + 2Ψ/c2 )c2 dt2 + (1 − 2Φ/c2 )dx2 .         (61)

The dynamics of slow bodies primarily measures Ψ, whereas gravitational lens-
ing depends on the combination Φ + Ψ. Therefore, a successful model must
simultaneously reproduce

         Vrot (r),   αlens (r),   cluster dynamics and structure growth.     (62)

Neither luminosity nor the internal charge content of neutral baryonic matter
by itself yields a derivation of (59). A calculation of the collective response of
the common causal-phase structure is required.

14 Elements Retained from Earlier Hypotheses

The following are retained from the causal-thermodynamic draft:

  • the primacy of causal order;

  • discrete U (1) holonomies;

  • separation of the base metric from the phase connection;

  • the possibility of an auxiliary KK representation;

  • the thermodynamic interpretation of the gravitational equation;

  • the requirement that cosmology be derived from the complete effective
    tensor rather than from analogies.

The following are retained from the causal null-network draft:

  • emphasis on null structure as a primary part of geometry;

  • the need to distinguish light cones from the metric rate of local clocks;

  • the idea of testing cosmology through light propagation;

  • the conjecture that galactic correlations may reflect a collective geometric
    response to the baryonic distribution.

15 Elements Excluded from the Core

The following claims from the earlier texts are not included in the new hypoth-
esis.

  1. Null radiation is not identified with a stretched string. Isotropic av-
     eraging of null dust gives w = +1/3, whereas w = −1/3 applies to a network
     of objects with longitudinal tension.

  2. Isotropization does not create a time axis. The condition ⟨ni ⟩ = 0 does
     not eliminate the second moment ⟨ni nj ⟩ and does not transform a spatial
     distribution into a purely temporal substrate.

  3. E ∝ N 2 is not postulated. The square of a sum of amplitudes in a coherent
     process is not a universal law for the total energy of a network.

                                       16

  4. Black holes are not treated as an external sink of the Universe’s
     energy. Their mass does not disappear from the exterior geometry; an
     open subsystem requires an explicit boundary and a definition of quasilo-
     cal energy.

  5. R = ct is not postulated as the observed history. Only the investigation
     of a⋆ ∝ t as a possible fixed point is admitted. The physical history must
     follow from Ω, qµν , and observational tests.

  6. A dynamical k(t) is not inserted into unchanged FLRW equations.
     Such a replacement changes the class of metrics.

  7. A free radion is not assumed. In the minimal version, the phase layer

For every analysis, publish:

18 Falsification Criteria

18.1 Common core

The common causal-phase hypothesis must be rejected or substantially modi-
fied if:

  1. a causal structure with local U (1) transports has no appropriate Lorentzian
     continuum limit;

  2. the discrete phase functional does not reproduce the Maxwell action;

                                       24

  3. causal order and the volume measure do not consistently reconstruct the
     same metric scale;

  4. joint coarse-graining violates gauge invariance, diffeomorphism invari-
     ance, or causality;

  5. the same effective functional cannot simultaneously describe the dynamics
     of massive bodies, lensing, and light propagation;

  6. the claimed galactic relations are fully explained by endogeneity of defini-
     tions, systematics, or ordinary astrophysics;

  7. cosmological observables cannot be reproduced by one consistent metric
     and one parameter set.

18.2 Physical-radion branch HR

The simple HR version is rejected if the parameter region required for a cos-
mological or plasma effect is inconsistent with MICROSCOPE, clocks, short-
distance gravity, scalar–photon conversion searches, and gravitational waves.
Separate screening mechanisms are admissible only when supplied by an ex-
plicit model with new testable consequences.

18.3 Phase-space branch HP

The HP branch loses empirical content if Γcorr can be selected independently for
each data set, or if coarse-graining leaves only renormalizations of G, e, and Λ
without new linked predictions. Detection of a universal free scalar pole moves
the model into the HR class or another physical scalar theory.

18.4 Fixed point a⋆ ∝ t

This additional hypothesis is rejected as physically useful if the reconstructed
deformation Ω(t) is merely an arbitrary reparameterization of the data, is not
derived from microdynamics, has no monotonic relation to an independent sta-
tistical functional, or requires violation of BAO, CMB, supernova time dilation,
and lensing.

19 Final Formulation

 The fundamental structure is assumed to be locally finite and causally or-
 dered. Causal order determines the null structure, volume information
 determines the metric scale, and U (1) holonomies determine the phase
 connection. Proper time is the length of a timelike worldline. Light is an
 excitation of the connection and a null probe of the full effective metric.
 Two competing interpretations of the phase layer remain. In HR , the
 layer is a physical five-dimensional geometry and contains a radion; in
 HP , it is an internal phase space without a free scalar mode. The minimal
 limit coincides with Einstein–Maxwell theory.
