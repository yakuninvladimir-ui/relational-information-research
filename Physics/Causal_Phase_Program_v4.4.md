## Abstract

The present specification formulates the mathematical apparatus for investigating how multiscale structures arise, maintain stability and interact in a discrete causal medium of limited bandwidth. The basic object is a finite partially ordered set of events (Bombelli et al., 1987) equipped with unitary phase transport on the immediate causal links (Hasse edges). The unified stochastic model — a complex Gaussian field whose precision matrix is the magnetic Laplacian of the phase-decorated graph — carries all statistical statements and the equations of the back-reaction of the field on the graph.

Any localised subsystem on a chosen time window and resolution scale is represented as a triad $(\mathcal{I}_A, \partial A, \mathcal{E}_A)$: an interior core, a two-sided interface of finite capacity, and an exterior synchronisation channel.

Eleven results are established with explicitly fixed logical status in $V_4$:
1. **Necessity of valence and diamond-covering constraints (§3.2).** The asymptotics of the number of partial orders on $N$ elements is dominated by two- and three-layer Kleitman–Rothschild graphs ($2^{N^2/4 + O(N)}$). No statistical weight depending only on $K, J, \|C\|_F$ and a linear chemical potential on the number of edges can suppress the entropy of the two-layer sector $\mathcal{T}_2$ (its suppression requires either the hard admissibility constraints (L0)–(L4), which reduce the entropy to $\exp(O(k_{\max}N\ln N))$, or a nonlinear penalty on vertex degree).
2. **Multiplicity of edge participation in diamonds $d_\diamond(x,y)$ (§2.1).** Written in closed matrix form, the multiplicity $d_\diamond(x,y)$ partitions Hasse edges into non-branching channels in oriented diamonds ($d_\diamond = 0$, $J_{xy}=0$) and diamond-embedded links ($d_\diamond \ge 1$). Complete gauge zeroing of phases on a subgraph with $d_\diamond=0$ without cycle frustration in $\Pi$ holds if and only if that subgraph contains no bipartite crowns $K_{2,2}$ (e.g., forms a tree/bridge in the undirected skeleton $R+R^\top$).
3. **Markov screening and the kernel-inclusion criterion (§2.4).** The equality $\Pi_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{E}} = 0$ holds identically by graph locality, the mediated covariance $\Sigma_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{E}}$ has an exact closed form, and a linear synchronisation map through the boundary exists if and only if $\ker\Pi_{\mathcal{I}b} \subseteq \ker\Pi_{\mathcal{E}b}$ (which is equivalent to a covering matching $\partial A \to \mathcal{I}_A$ for typical phases).
4. **Conditional selection of $K^{\mathrm{eff}}_{xy} = 2$ and encoding of dimension by diamond density (§3.3–3.4).** Binary diamonds minimise the convex branching functional at fixed frustration $J_0 > 0$; moreover the oriented hypercube $H_d$ of any dimension $d \ge 2$ consists exclusively of binary diamonds ($K_{\max} = 2$) with density $\binom{d}{2}$ per interior vertex.
5. **Helmholtz decomposition, invariance of the static measure and localisation of the causal arrow in $Q_W$ (§2.4, §3.5).** The Hermitian part of the transport $W + W^\dagger$ forms the precision matrix $\Pi$, for which $\Pi(R^\top,\Theta^\top) = \overline{\Pi(R,\Theta)}$, so $\ln\det\Pi$, $\mathscr{S}_{\mathrm{interface}}$ and $W_{\mathrm{cross}}$ are independent of reversal of the causal order. The causal arrow enters dynamically through the skew-Hermitian solenoidal flux $Q_W = \frac{\beta}{2}(W - W^\dagger)$, which changes sign under edge inversion, preserves the stationary Boltzmann measure $\mathcal{CN}(0, \Pi^{-1})$ for any $\beta$ and increases the spectral relaxation gap $\gamma(Q_W) \ge \gamma(0)$.
6. **Exact entropy balance (§4).** For a random dynamical system with additive noise and an injective $C^1$ map $G$, stationarity requires the exact equality $\Delta_{\mathrm{noise}} = -\mathbb{E}\ln|\det DG| = -\sum_i \lambda_i = \kappa - h_+$.
7. **Multiscale synchronisation and realisation of boundary spectra (§5).** Stochastic realisation of rational boundary spectra in state space and suppression of fast modes $O((\gamma_k\tau_w^{(\ell)})^{-1})$ are proved on a non-empty window $\tau_w^{(\ell)} \in (\gamma_3^{-1}, \gamma_2^{-1})$; the synchronisation time of a homogeneous $d$-dimensional ball diverges as $\Theta(N^{3/d})$, competing with regular expanders ($\tau_{\mathrm{sync}} = O(\ln N)$) and filamentary networks in Protocol V5.
8. **Field back-reaction, Woodbury formula and loop expansion (§6.1–6.2).** Integration of $n_{\mathrm{bos}}$ components of a bosonic field at temperature $T$ induces the contribution $+\eta_{\mathrm{field}}\ln\det\Pi(R,\Theta)$ ($\eta_{\mathrm{field}} = n_{\mathrm{bos}} T$), for which rank-1 factorisation, the $2\times 2$ Woodbury law under edge replacement and a convergent loop expansion with sensitivity threshold $\ell = 4$ (diamonds and crowns $K_{2,2}$) and dominance of long loops $\ell \ge 6$ at moderate masses $m^2 \lesssim 1$ are proved.
9. **Wilson cross-action identity and area law (§6.3–6.4).** From the triadic Schur decomposition the identity $W_{\mathrm{cross}}(A; \Theta) = \mathscr{S}_{\mathrm{interface}}(A; 0) - \mathscr{S}_{\mathrm{interface}}(A; \Theta) = -\Delta\mathscr{S}_{\mathrm{interface}}(A; \Theta) \ge 0$ is proved analytically and the local quadratic polarisation of the boundary $a_{\mathrm{eff}}(B) = a_0 - \kappa_\sigma(m^2)B^2 + O(B^4)$ is studied.
10. **Inter-level algebra under Schur–Haar projection and the screened kernel subspace (§7).** Upon Haar averaging over the group $SU(k)$ of interior gauges of the core in $\mathfrak{su}(k+m)$ the interior modes $\mathfrak{su}(k)$ and the cross modes vanish, while the fixed subspace is isomorphic to $\mathfrak{u}(1)_{\mathrm{inter}} \oplus \mathfrak{su}(m)_\partial$ with unique Killing-orthogonal centre generator $Q_{\mathrm{orth}} = \operatorname{diag}(\frac{1}{k}I_k, -\frac{1}{m}I_m)$ (and terminal generator $Q_v = \operatorname{diag}(\frac{1}{k}I_k, -vv^\dagger)$ under rank-1 splicing). When $|\mathcal{I}_A| > |\partial A|$ the subspace $\Pi_{\mathcal{I}}(\ker\Pi_{b\mathcal{I}})$ of dimension $\ge |\mathcal{I}_A| - |\partial A|$ has strictly zero unconditional covariance with the exterior zone $\mathcal{E}_A$.
11. **Phase $\epsilon$-admissibility (L5), continuum interpolation of Lüscher–Witten and chiral fermions (§8).** The condition $|U_\square - 1| < \epsilon < 2\sin\frac{\pi}{2(d-1)}$ (equivalent to $J_{xy} < \epsilon^2/2$ on binary diamonds) allows unique interpolation of the discrete phases $\theta_{xy}$ into a continuum 1-form $A_\mu(x)$ by solving linear Dirichlet–Landau problems on the cells of hypercubes and Freudenthal simplices and defines the partition function of chiral fermions according to the Witten scheme (`arXiv:2611.05051`).

## 0. Methodological Standard, Geometric Core of $V_4$ and Stratification of the OMIT Kernel

### 0.1. Projection Triad, Insignificance Kernel and Confidence Ellipsoid $B_t$

Let $X$ be the space of elementary transitions (or shifts of observables) of the system on a time window $\tau_w$ and resolution scale $\ell$.

**Definition 0.1 (Boundary contract and three-valued ontological partition).** `[Status: FOLLOW]`  
A verification contract (law or scale description) $K$ is given by a projection operator $\pi_K : X \to Y_K$ onto the factor space of essential features $Y_K$ with a distinguished zero element $0 \in Y_K$, which partitions $X$ into three disjoint ontological domains in the sense of Brusentsov three-valued logic:
$$X = R_K^0 \sqcup R_K^+ \sqcup R_K^\perp.$$
1. **Insignificance kernel (domain `OMIT`, status $0$):** $R_K^0 := \ker \pi_K = \{x \in X \mid \pi_K(x) = 0\}$, containing all transitions that are invariant or inapplicable with respect to the law $K$ (non-activated operators $a_k = 0$, contexts $c_k = 0$ blocked by an external obstacle, internal motions orthogonal to the law, and declared sub-threshold fluctuations $\Omega_{\mathrm{sub},K}$).
2. **Target cone of consequence (domain `FOLLOW`, status $+1$):** $R_K^+ := \{x \in X \setminus \ker \pi_K \mid \pi_K(x) \in C_K^+\}$, where $\varnothing \neq C_K^+ \subset Y_K \setminus \{0\}$ is a non-empty non-universal target cone of productive shift.
3. **Domain of incompatibility / annulment (domain `NULL`, status $-1$):** $R_K^\perp := X \setminus (R_K^0 \cup R_K^+) = \{x \in X \setminus \ker \pi_K \mid \pi_K(x) \notin C_K^+\}$, collecting all extra-kernel transitions that violate the contract (sign inversion along active axes, violation of the admissibility invariants (L0)–(L4), exit beyond the tolerance).

**Definition 0.2 (Observational confidence ellipsoid $B_t$).** `[Status: FOLLOW]`  
At finite observational resolution or finite number of Monte-Carlo sample trajectories a transition is represented not by a point but by a non-empty compact confidence set $B_t \subseteq X$:
* Under exact deterministic computation (identical Phase-0 checks) $B_t = \{x_t\}$ collapses to a point;
* Under statistical estimation with mean $\hat{x}_t$, error covariance $\Sigma_{\mathrm{epi}}$ and significance threshold $\gamma_{\mathrm{snr}} > 0$ (in protocols V1–V8 the threshold $\gamma_{\mathrm{snr}} = 3.0\sigma$ is fixed) the set is a Mahalanobis ellipsoid $B_t = \{x : \|x - \hat{x}_t\|_{\Sigma_{\mathrm{epi}}^{-1}} \le \gamma_{\mathrm{snr}}\}$;
* In the absence of an authoritative measurement (`PREDICTIVE_ONLY`, `NOT_AVAILABLE`) or without a valid certificate the ball inflates to the whole space: $B_t = X$.

### 0.2. Theorem on the Four Verdicts $V_4$ and the Nature of UNDECIDED

**Theorem 0.1 (Topological generation of $V_4$ from three-valued ontology).** `[Status: FOLLOW]`  
For any non-empty confidence set $B_t \subseteq X$ the following four conditions are pairwise incompatible, exhaust all possible configurations of $B_t$ and define a total deterministic single-valued map into $V_4 = \{\mathrm{FOLLOW}\ (+1),\, \mathrm{OMIT}\ (0),\, \mathrm{NULL}\ (-1),\, \mathrm{UNDECIDED}\ (\mathrm{U})\}$:
* $\mathrm{Verdict}_K(B_t) = \mathrm{OMIT}\ (0)$ if $B_t \subseteq R_K^0 \iff \pi_K(B_t) = \{0\}$;
* $\mathrm{Verdict}_K(B_t) = \mathrm{FOLLOW}\ (+1)$ if $B_t \subseteq R_K^+ \iff \pi_K(B_t) \subseteq C_K^+$;
* $\mathrm{Verdict}_K(B_t) = \mathrm{NULL}\ (-1)$ if $B_t \subseteq R_K^\perp \iff \pi_K(B_t) \cap (C_K^+ \cup \{0\}) = \varnothing$;
* $\mathrm{Verdict}_K(B_t) = \mathrm{UNDECIDED}\ (\mathrm{U})$ in all remaining cases (there exist $u \neq v \in \{0, +, \perp\}$ such that $B_t \cap R_K^u \neq \varnothing$ and $B_t \cap R_K^v \neq \varnothing$).

**Corollary 0.1 (Nature of UNDECIDED and two paths of its resolution).** `[Status: FOLLOW]`  
`UNDECIDED` ($\mathrm{U}$) is not a fourth ontological truth-value; it is a geometric indicator that the confidence ball $B_t$ intersects the boundary between ontological domains (the radius of statistical uncertainty exceeds the distance from the centre of the ball to the domain boundary). Resolution of `UNDECIDED` is possible by exactly two routes:
1. Contraction of the confidence ball $B_t' \subseteq B_t$ (increase of the number of independent seeds, relaxation steps or passage to a parameter region with high signal-to-noise ratio);
2. Change of the coarsening scale $\ell \to \ell'$ (expansion of the kernel $\ker \pi_K \subseteq \ker \pi_{K'}$, absorbing boundary fluctuations, while respecting the MBEL re-verification protocol §5.4).

**Proposition 0.2 (Monotonicity of refinement, asymmetry of refutation and the non-promotion rule).** `[Status: FOLLOW]`
1. **Stability of resolved verdicts:** if $\varnothing \neq B' \subseteq B$ and $\mathrm{Verdict}_K(B) \in \{\mathrm{FOLLOW}, \mathrm{OMIT}, \mathrm{NULL}\}$, then $\mathrm{Verdict}_K(B') = \mathrm{Verdict}_K(B)$.
2. **Asymmetry of authoritative refutation:** upon receipt of a typed certificate with admissible set $Z_C$ the ball contracts to $B_t \cap Z_C$. A refusal certificate with $B_t \cap Z_C \subseteq R_K^\perp$ (e.g., failure of any single null-test) transfers the verdict to `NULL` even when the a-priori ball $B_t$ intersects the boundary, whereas for `FOLLOW` the whole ball $B_t \subseteq R_K^+$ is required.
3. **Non-promotion rule (MBEL §5.4):** a status `UNDECIDED` fixed upon exhaustion of the computational budget (including borderline values $2.9\sigma < 3.0\sigma$) is never overwritten to `NULL` or `FOLLOW` without new independent data, and the contract threshold cannot be relaxed retrospectively to fit an exploratory sample.

### 0.3. Stratification of the Insignificance Kernel: Three Operational Subtypes of OMIT

According to Corollary 2.2 of the *Boundary Projection Theorem* and MBEL §5.2, when $\pi_K(B_t) = \{0\}$ the position of the set $B_t$ inside the clause-stratified kernel $\ker \pi_K$ distinguishes three disjoint subtypes of the status `OMIT` (in order of controller priority):

1. **`OMIT_BLOCKED`** (active operator $a_k = 1$ blocked by precondition $c_k = 0$): the protocol or transition is called but is stopped at the boundary of the admissible region before measurement is performed (e.g., rejection of launch of Protocol V2 by the input gateway of Lemma 4.2 when $g \|H_A\|_2 \ge 1 - \delta$, or skipping of a pairwise interaction in V4 upon receiving `NULL` in V3).
2. **`OMIT_REDUNDANT`** (identical zero shift $B_t = \{0_X\}$): absence of change of the descriptor or a tautological consequence of the definitions (e.g., the identity $K_{xy} = 0$ on a direct Hasse edge $R_{xy} = 1$).
3. **`OMIT_INCIDENTAL`** (motion inside $B_t \subseteq \ker \pi_K \setminus \{0_X\}$ when $a_k = 0$ or in the sub-threshold zone $\Omega_{\mathrm{sub},K}$): real dynamics occurs in the system that is strictly orthogonal to the law $K$ under examination. In the present specification the following four fundamental matrix kernels belong to `OMIT_INCIDENTAL`:
   * local gauge rotations $D_\chi = \operatorname{diag}(e^{i\chi_x})$, which do not change gauge-invariant observables (§1.2, §1.5);
   * the anti-Hermitian solenoidal luminosity flux $Q_W = \frac{\beta}{2}(W - W^\dagger)$, which preserves the stationary covariance $\Sigma = \Pi^{-1}$ and the determinant $\ln\det\Pi$ (§3.5, Proposition 3.6);
   * traceless interior modes $G_{k\times k} \in \mathfrak{su}(k)$ and off-diagonal transfer modes $X_{k\times m}$, annihilated by the Schur–Haar coarsening projector $\mathcal{R}_{\ell \to \ell+1}^{\mathrm{Haar}}$ (§7.1, Proposition 7.1);
   * linear functionals of interior core modes $v^\dagger \psi_{\mathcal{I}}$ with $v \in \Pi_{\mathcal{I}}(\ker \Pi_{b\mathcal{I}})$ under rank overflow of the boundary $|\mathcal{I}_A| > |\partial A|$, which have strictly zero unconditional covariance $v^\dagger \Sigma_{\mathcal{I}\mathcal{E}} = 0$ with the exterior field $\psi_{\mathcal{E}}$ (§7.3, Proposition 7.3).

### 0.4. Dominance Semilattice and Cumulative Aggregation Operator $\mathrm{sLSE}_T$

When an ensemble of contracts $\{K_1, \dots, K_M\}$ is verified simultaneously the basic verdicts are ordered by a strict linear dominance chain:
$$\mathrm{NULL}\ (-1) \;\succ_V\; \mathrm{UNDECIDED}\ (\mathrm{U}) \;\succ_V\; \mathrm{OMIT}\ (0) \;\succ_V\; \mathrm{FOLLOW}\ (+1), \qquad u \otimes v := \max_{\succ_V}(u, v).$$
On the simplex of soft weights $p = (p^{(+)}, p^{(0)}, p^{(\mathrm{U})}, p^{(-)})^\top \in \Delta^3$ the bijection into cumulative strictness coordinates:
$$\Phi_{\mathrm{cum}}(p) = q := \bigl[ p^{(-)},\; p^{(-)} + p^{(\mathrm{U})},\; p^{(-)} + p^{(\mathrm{U})} + p^{(0)} \bigr]^\top \in \mathcal{Q} = \{0 \le q_1 \le q_2 \le q_3 \le 1\}$$
translates the operation $\otimes$ into the coordinate-wise maximum $(\bigvee_m q_m)_j = \max_m q_{m,j}$, and its smooth relaxation at temperature $T > 0$ into the padding-invariant shifted operator:
$$\mathrm{sLSE}_T(q_{1,j}, \dots, q_{M,j}) := \min\!\left\{ 1,\; T \ln\!\left( 1 + \sum_{m=1}^M \bigl(\exp(q_{m,j} / T) - 1\bigr) \right) \right\}.$$
The operator $\mathrm{sLSE}_T$ possesses three exact properties: (1) inertness of `FOLLOW` (a satisfied contract with $q_m = (0,0,0)^\top$ contributes $e^0 - 1 = 0$ and does not distort the sum for any $M$); (2) absorption of `NULL` (a single reliable `NULL` with $q_m = (1,1,1)^\top$ monotonically dominates the sum); (3) protection against empty truth (multiplication by the activity guard $p_{\mathrm{prog}}(E)$ guarantees that a step without active checks receives `OMIT`, not `FOLLOW`).
